Signet Development was founded by Iain Galloway in 2011 to help organisations build and change critical software with clear governance processes.

## One practice for critical change

Technical leadership sets direction and brings stakeholders together around important decisions. Digital transformation modernises or replaces critical systems while protecting operations. Delivery governance enables change that is prompt and accountable.

## From intent to outcome

Iain builds relationships, processes, and systems to engage stakeholders throughout delivery to keep the intended outcome clear and decisions aligned with it. Rationale, progress, and operational results remain continuously visible as the work evolves. That visibility equips teams to use evidence and feedback to steer delivery and address divergence early.
