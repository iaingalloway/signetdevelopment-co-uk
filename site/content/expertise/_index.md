Signet Development helps organisations build and change software they depend on. Iain Galloway leads the work, bringing engineers, operations teams and decision-makers together.

## Technical leadership

Teams need a clear goal, technical direction and decisions they can act on. That means understanding what the organisation needs, choosing an approach that fits, and helping people work through the consequences.

Iain takes responsibility for architecture and technical delivery. He works with senior leaders on the business case, recruits and leads engineering teams, and develops the skills needed to run the finished system. He can lead an existing client team or build a team for the project.

## Digital transformation

An ageing system can become expensive to run, difficult to change or an obstacle to the organisation's plans. Replacing it still has to account for the people and operations that depend on it.

The work can include redesigning software, moving services to the cloud and changing how systems connect. The choice follows the business need, the cost of running the service and the team's ability to maintain it.

## Delivery governance

Manual releases, repeated data entry and approvals scattered across emails make changes slow and prone to error.

Iain sets up processes in which proposed changes are visible, the people responsible can review and approve them, and testing and deployment are automated. The process records what changed and who approved it, without relying on someone to reconstruct the history afterwards.

These are connected parts of the same work: deciding what to build, leading the people building it, and making sure they can change and operate it reliably.
